No. The decoder is trained from scratch on content (COCO) and style (WikiArt) datasets. The VGG encoder is a pre-trained feature extractor — consistent with the Huang & Belongie (2017) AdaIN paper. The engineering, training, productization, and UI are the author's original implementation.

AdaIN aligns the mean and standard deviation of content features with those of style features, transferring the style's statistics in a single forward pass — unlike iterative optimization methods. This makes it real-time (single inference call, no gradient loop).

Alpha controls interpolation between content and stylized features: output = alpha × AdaIN(content, style) + (1 - alpha) × content. At 0, you get the original content; at 1, you get maximum stylization.
